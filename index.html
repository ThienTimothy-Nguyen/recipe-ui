Crispy Korean Fried Chicken

"The only recipe you will ever need."

Ingredients

  • Whole chicken
  • Rice wine
  • Minced ginger
  • Sea salt
  • Ground black pepper
  • Potato starch
  • Cooking oil

Method

  1. In a bowl, combine the chicken, rice wine, ginger, salt, and black pepper.
  2. Coat the chicken evenly with starch.
  3. Heat a generous amount of oil in a deep saucepan or fryer to 175°C / 347°F. Carefully add the chicken in batches.
  4. Fry each batch for 3–5 minutes. Transfer the chicken to paper towels.
  5. Scoop any floating debris from the oil. When it returns to 175°C / 347°F, fry the chicken a second time for 2–3 minutes.
  6. Serve as is, or toss with an optional Korean fried chicken sauce.
